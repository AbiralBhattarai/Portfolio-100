# Home (one-page portfolio)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: hiring managers. Action: Email or Download CV, equal weight. Build path: code-led (no image generation).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio as a product launch page. Abiral's work is presented the way a flagship product is launched: one headline at a time, huge bold type, one product demonstrated in motion. Refuses the engineer default of name, tagline, and a grid of project cards.

OWN-WORLD: White ground (#fff) and soft grey panels (#f5f5f7) with near-black ink; one black chapter for Sectra with a lime signal (#c8ff2e) for speech, words and clips; ultramarine (#2b3bff) for every action and the closing section, drenched. Schibsted Grotesk at 800–900 for display with tight tracking, 400–600 for body; Fragment Mono only for timestamps and data (Geist was replaced after the detector flagged it as overused). Light and dark themes per Abiral's request: dark ground #131316, panels #1c1c20; the Sectra chapter stays #000 with hairline edges. Pill buttons, hairline rules, no cards.

STORY: In seconds the visitor reads "AI × Product", sees Sectra cut a clip from a waveform, and has Email and CV in reach. Scrolling, they watch Sectra's six-stage pipeline (ending in one-click auto-post), read the AI × product research topics, follow the open-source agent's ReAct-loop graph, scan other work (Quickfox rows unlinked, GitHub rows linked), specs-style skills separated by |, and the timeline, then close on a drenched contact section. Veel appears only as role titles in the timeline; no Veel section.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim nav: name, GitHub, LinkedIn, Email, a Resume pill, theme toggle. Headline "AI × Product." at up to 13.5rem, × in ultramarine, spanning the width. One-sentence intro and two equal pills (Email me, View CV; the CV opens in a new tab) below it. Along the bottom edge, a full-width waveform band where speech is ink, the clip lights ultramarine (lime fails contrast on white), words rise, and a clip bracket snaps, captioned "Sectra finding a clip".

FORM: seed: 505de7356e2bdc59 (rolled; the bolder re-roll was then overridden mid-round by Abiral's style pin: "applesque with focus on bold fonts", "what a really creative web designer's website looks like"; first viewport and structure authored to that pin). Signature interaction: sticky scroll through Sectra's six stages in one stage panel. Motion grammar: exponential ease-out, scroll-stepped, reduced-motion shows the final states.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
